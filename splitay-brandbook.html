Logo — primary

Concept T3, Quartered S. Bricolage Grotesque 700 at text optical size, cut once vertically and once horizontally. Each quarter carries one flat brand colour — no gradients, no tints. Two approved lockup colourways: on light Ink / Tide 400 / Ember 500 / Ember 400 · on dark White / Tide 400 / Ember 500 / Ember 400. The app icon uses U1 on the Tide field: White / Tide 300 / Ember 100 / Ember 400. Quartered at 24px and up; below that the two-colour kerf mark, Ink for 1-colour. On Ember the mark goes solid white.

Tone of voice
“Short sentences. Confident. Never corporate.”
Say the number first, the explanation second. Use “you” and first names, never “the user”. Money words stay plain: owes, paid, settled. No exclamation marks in balances — only in celebrations.

Groups Chat Bot — states
Not connected
Show paywall card + 3-step instructions. CTA “Connect a new chat”.
Pending pairing
Pairing code valid 10 min, amber chip, live countdown, “Resend code”.
Live
Green chip, member count, per-chat mute and “Unlink”.
Parse failed
Bot replies in chat with the expected format; app shows an inbox item to fix manually.
Quota reached
200/mo on Plus. Bot posts one warning at 90%, then stops silently; app shows upgrade banner.
Subscription lapsed
Chats stay linked read-only for 14 days, then unlink. Existing expenses never disappear.
